[Confluence Coffee](/directory/eat-drink/confluence-coffee/) opened on the High Street to catch the morning walk to Brentford and Kew Bridge stations, and it has become one of the town's reliable independents.

The café roasts to order, keeps the menu tight, and knows its regulars by name — the things a chain cannot fake. It signed up as a founding Brentford Card business because the maths of independent coffee is unforgiving and every extra morning visit counts.

## The offer

Brentford Card members get a free pastry with any coffee before 10am, one per member per day, dine-in. It rewards the habit the café most wants to build: a proper sit-down start to the day rather than a takeaway cup on the move.
