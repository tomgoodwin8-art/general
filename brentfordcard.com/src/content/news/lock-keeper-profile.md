[The Lock Keeper](/directory/eat-drink/the-lock-keeper/) sits where the Grand Union Canal meets the tideway, and it is one of the founding businesses backing the Brentford Card.

The pub leans into its setting — a short, changing menu, local beer, and a terrace that fills the moment the sun is out. It is the sort of independent the card is built to support: rooted in one place, run by people who live nearby, and reliant on regulars rather than passing trade.

## The offer

Founding members get 10% off food Monday to Thursday. It is not a gimmick discount; it is a nudge to make the local your local on the quieter nights when independents most need the covers.
