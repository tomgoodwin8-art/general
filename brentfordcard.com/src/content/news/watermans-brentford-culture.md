[Watermans](/directory/culture/watermans-arts-centre/) has anchored culture in Brentford for decades, and it is one of the founding venues on the Brentford Card.

Sitting on the riverbank at the eastern end of the High Street, Watermans runs a cinema, a theatre programme, exhibitions and a café-bar. It is exactly the kind of institution a local card should reward: open to everyone, rooted in the town, and better the more people through the doors.

## The offer

Members get £2 off cinema tickets, one per member per screening, subject to availability. A night at the pictures is one of the easiest ways to feel the card paying for itself.
