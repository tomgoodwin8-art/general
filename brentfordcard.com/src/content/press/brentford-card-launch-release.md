**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE — Brentford, London, 15 July 2026**

The Brentford Card, a community platform for the TW8 postcode, has launched today with a paid annual membership card that lives in Apple and Google Wallet.

For £29 a year, members get discounts and offers at independent businesses across Brentford, from canalside pubs to the High Street's cafés, shops and the Watermans arts centre. Five per cent of every membership goes to a named Brentford cause.

The scheme is independent — it is not run by the council and is not developer-funded. The first 250 memberships are offered as founding cards at a locked-in price.

"Brentford is gaining thousands of new neighbours as The Brentford Project and Kew Bridge Rise complete," said a spokesperson for the Brentford Card. "The card is the simplest way we could think of to send that new spending to the independents who make this a place worth living."

The Brentford Card is available now at brentfordcard.com/card. Businesses in TW8 can list for free during launch.
