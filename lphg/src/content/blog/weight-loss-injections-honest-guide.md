## What weight loss injections actually are

"Weight loss injections" almost always means a **GLP-1 receptor agonist** — a
medicine that mimics a natural gut hormone, glucagon-like peptide-1. The two in
widest use are semaglutide (Wegovy) and tirzepatide (Mounjaro, which also acts on
a second hormone, GIP). They are given as a once-weekly injection under the skin.

They are not fat-dissolving injections, and they are not a shortcut around
nutrition and movement. They work by changing appetite.

## What they show works — and what they cannot do

GLP-1 medicines reduce hunger, increase fullness after meals, and slow how
quickly the stomach empties. For most people that means eating less without the
constant battle against appetite. What they cannot do is replace the assessment,
monitoring and maintenance planning that make weight loss safe and durable.

## Why a prescriber-led service matters

These are prescription-only medicines for good reason. A proper service checks
your BMI and history, screens for conditions where they are unsafe, starts at a
low dose and titrates upward to manage side effects, and monitors you over time.
Buying online without assessment skips every one of those safeguards.

## How to prepare for your consultation

Bring a list of your current medicines, any recent blood results, and a sense of
what you have already tried. If you have a personal or family history of thyroid
or pancreatic disease, tell your prescriber — it changes what is safe to offer.

## Understanding the costs

The honest market picture: prescriber-led GLP-1 services in London typically run
from around £150 to £250 for an initial consultation, with medication charged
separately. Prices vary widely, and the cheapest online options usually skip the
clinical assessment that keeps you safe.

## The metabolic bigger picture

Excess weight rarely travels alone. Fatty liver, raised blood pressure and
disordered glucose often move together. Many patients pair a weight consultation
with a baseline check of liver and metabolic health, which is why our
weight-management pathway sits alongside imaging and blood testing rather than in
isolation.
