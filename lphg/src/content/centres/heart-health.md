  - q: Do I need a GP referral for a heart scan?

  - q: What is the difference between an ECG and an echocardiogram?
    a: An ECG records the heart's electrical activity and helps detect rhythm problems, while an echocardiogram is an ultrasound that shows the heart's structure, valves and pumping function. Some people benefit from both.
