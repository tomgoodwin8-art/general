faqs:
  - q: How early can I have a pregnancy scan?
    a: A meaningful early pregnancy scan is usually possible from around six weeks, when a heartbeat can often be seen. Before this it may be too early to give a clear result, and we may suggest rebooking in a week or two.
  - q: Do I need a GP referral for a pregnancy scan?
    a: No. You can book an early pregnancy or dating scan directly. If your scan raises anything that needs further care, we can refer you to an obstetric team.
  - q: Will the scan be abdominal or internal?
    a: This depends on how many weeks pregnant you are. In very early pregnancy a transvaginal (internal) scan often gives the clearest view. Your sonographer will explain the options and only proceed with your consent.
