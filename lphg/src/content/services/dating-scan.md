A dating scan measures your baby in the first trimester to give an accurate
estimate of your due date and confirm the pregnancy is developing as expected.
It is the most reliable point to date a pregnancy.

At LPHG the scan is performed by an experienced sonographer, with a written report
issued the same day and shared with your maternity team on request.
