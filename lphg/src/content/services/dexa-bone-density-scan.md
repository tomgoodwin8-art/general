Our DEXA bone density scan measures the strength of your bones at the hip and
spine, helping to detect osteoporosis and assess your risk of fracture.

The scan is quick, painless and uses a very low dose of X-rays. A consultant
reports the results, comparing your bone density against expected values and
advising on any treatment or monitoring.
