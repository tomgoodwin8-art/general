An early pregnancy scan offers reassurance in the first weeks by confirming the
pregnancy is in the right place and checking for a heartbeat. It can also help
explain early bleeding or pain.

At LPHG the scan is performed by an experienced sonographer, with a written report
issued the same day. If any concern arises, we can arrange prompt onward care.
