  - q: "Is an echocardiogram the same as an ECG?"
    a: >-
      No. An echocardiogram is an ultrasound that shows the heart's structure
      and pumping, while an ECG records its electrical activity. The two tests
      are complementary and are often done together.
  - q: "Does the scan hurt?"

      moves a probe over it. You will be asked to lie on your left side and
      breathe normally.

    a: >-
      Not directly. It assesses the heart muscle and valves rather than the
      coronary arteries. If artery disease is suspected, the cardiologist may
      recommend a further test such as a CT coronary angiogram.
