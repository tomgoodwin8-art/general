A fibroid ultrasound is used to diagnose and measure fibroids, and to explain
symptoms such as heavy periods, pelvic pressure or a feeling of fullness. It
records the size and position of each fibroid so change can be tracked over time.

At LPHG the scan is performed and reported by a consultant radiologist, with a
written report available within 24 hours. If treatment is worth considering, we
can refer you to a consultant gynaecologist.
