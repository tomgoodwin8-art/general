Our knee ultrasound assesses the tendons, ligaments and soft tissues around the
joint, and is particularly useful for swellings such as a Baker's cyst.

Where the menisci or cruciate ligaments need to be assessed, the radiologist
will advise on an MRI, which we can arrange.
