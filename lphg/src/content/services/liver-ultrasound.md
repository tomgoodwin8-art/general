    a: >-

Our liver ultrasound focuses on the liver and bile ducts, and is a common
first-line test for fatty liver and abnormal liver blood results.

Where scarring needs to be assessed, it pairs naturally with a FibroScan, which
measures liver stiffness without a biopsy.
