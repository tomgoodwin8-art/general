Our MSK ultrasound gives a real-time view of the soft tissues around a painful
or injured joint, in the Harley Street Medical District.

Because the scan is dynamic, the radiologist can watch tendons and muscles move,
which often pinpoints the cause of pain more clearly than a static image.
