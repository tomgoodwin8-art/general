  - q: Can an ultrasound diagnose PCOS by itself?
    a: "No. PCOS is diagnosed when at least two of three features are present: irregular periods, raised androgens, and polycystic ovaries on ultrasound. The scan assesses the third feature."
  - q: Why is a transvaginal scan recommended for PCOS?

A PCOS ultrasound looks at the ovaries for the pattern of small follicles seen
in polycystic ovary syndrome. It forms one part of the assessment, alongside your
symptoms and blood tests.

At LPHG the scan is performed and reported by a consultant radiologist, with a
written report available within 24 hours. We can arrange the blood tests and a
gynaecology consultation to complete the picture.
