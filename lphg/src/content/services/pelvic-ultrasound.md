A pelvic ultrasound is the first-line scan for investigating pelvic pain,
abnormal bleeding, or a suspected cyst or fibroid. It gives a clear picture of
the uterus and ovaries without radiation.

At LPHG the scan is performed and reported by a consultant radiologist, with a
written report available within 24 hours. If anything needs onward care, we can
refer you to a consultant gynaecologist.
