A prostate assessment brings together a symptom review, a PSA blood test and an
examination to give you a clear picture of your prostate health and cancer risk,
with a plan for anything that needs following up.

At LPHG the assessment is consultant-led, with a written summary within 24 hours.
If further imaging or referral is advised, we can arrange it promptly.
