A testicular ultrasound is the first-line scan for investigating a lump,
swelling or pain in the scrotum. It gives a clear, painless picture of both
testicles and the surrounding structures.

At LPHG the scan is performed and reported by a consultant radiologist, with a
written report within 24 hours. If a finding needs specialist care, we can arrange
prompt onward referral.
