Our thyroid ultrasound is a detailed, painless scan of the thyroid gland and
neck, used to assess nodules, swelling and other changes.

The scan is performed by a specialist and reported by a consultant, and is
often combined with a thyroid function blood test for a fuller picture of both
structure and function.
