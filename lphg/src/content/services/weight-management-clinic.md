A weight management consultation is a doctor-led review of your health and goals,
setting out your options across diet, activity and, where suitable, prescription
medication. The aim is a realistic, personalised plan.

At LPHG the consultation is led by Dr Ali Aghaei, with a written summary and plan
within 24 hours and a clear follow-up schedule.
