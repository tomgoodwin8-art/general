A well woman screen brings a consultation and a pelvic ultrasound together in one
visit, giving you a clear review of your gynaecological health and a plan for
anything that needs following up.

At LPHG the screen is consultant-led, with a written summary available within 24
hours. If a finding needs specialist care, we can arrange onward referral.
