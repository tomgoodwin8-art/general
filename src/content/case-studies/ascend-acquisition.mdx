## The situation

As Managing Director of Ascend Global, I took the agency through an acquisition
by MOOT Group, completed in February 2022. That meant getting the P&L and the
operation into a shape a buyer would pay for, then running the buyer conversations
and the negotiation itself.

## What I did

Tidied the operational story, framed the numbers honestly, and led the
back-and-forth to a completed deal. Selling an agency is a different discipline
from running one: the things that make day-to-day delivery work are not always
the things a buyer values.

## The result

A completed acquisition. This is the experience behind
[Agency Exit Readiness](/agency-exit-readiness): advising agency owners who are
preparing to sell, from positioning the P&L to the final negotiation.
