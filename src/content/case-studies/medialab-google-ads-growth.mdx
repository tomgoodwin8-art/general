## The situation

As Search Director at Medialab, the UK's largest independent media agency, I led
search across a portfolio managing £20m+ in paid media. The mandate: grow Google
Ads performance without growing waste.

## What I did

Built a measurement doctrine led by MER rather than last-click, rebuilt account
architecture around broad match and Smart Bidding where it earned its place, and
held the team to incrementality thinking. Disciplined structure over clever
tactics.

## The result

60% year-on-year Google Ads growth and Google Premier Partner status, earned in
February 2024. The same measurement-first approach underpins every
[paid media](/work-with-me/paid-media-strategy) engagement.
